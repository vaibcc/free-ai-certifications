## Important Information

Not every course listed provides a free professional certification.

Credentials may include:

- **Free certification:** An official certification earned after meeting the provider's requirements.
- **Digital badge:** A shareable digital credential.
- **Completion certificate:** Recognition for completing a course.
- **Free learning:** Course material is free, but a certificate or examination may require payment.

Some providers change their pricing, examination fees, eligibility requirements, and certificate policies. Always check the official course page before enrolling.
